# KryptoLens

See what matters in crypto.

KryptoLens compiles human information intent into a continuously executable Intelligence Policy. It is an intent-driven crypto intelligence system, not a generic AI chatbot or traditional market dashboard.

## Platform

web

## Register

product

## Who it is for

An operator who can name a market condition in language and needs a receipt they can trust — scored from CoinMarketCap, not a chat transcript.

## Scene

Night desk. A researcher scans a dark surface, checks one event, and decides whether to trust it. Quiet moss light, no tape glow.

## Voice

Direct. Specific. Never claims real-time or literal continuous observation. Monitoring happens every 15 minutes. Never calls itself a demo.

## Surfaces

Landing (composer preview) → Enter Demo → Workspace conversation → New Lens → Activate → async Run now → Event Receipt → Settings.

## Out of scope

Trading, charts, wallets, Discover, search, notification bell, frequency pickers, extra agents, LLM scoring.
