# Brag Plan: KryptoLens

## What is this app?
Tell KryptoLens what matters. Give it a job. It keeps watch for you.

## The angle
polished

## Hook
Positioned as a persistent crypto intelligence teammate, not a chatbot, dashboard, demo, or trading bot. Voice: Tell → Define → Watch → Act → Return. Quiet markets are a valid result.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Tell KryptoLens what matters. Give it a job. It keeps watch for you.
- Someone who can name a crypto-market job in language wants a teammate that stays on it, not a dashboard they have to configure.
- A Lens is a persistent crypto agent with a compiled job, workflow, routines, tool permissions, conversation, and results; AgentRuntime plans then LensRuntime/workflow hits live CMC and writes verified results back.
- A Lens is a user-owned persistent crypto agent with name, purpose, natural-language request, status (draft/active/paused), versions, routines, conversation, and results.

## Outro / punchline
KryptoLens
