From question to evidence

Every answer has a trail.

KryptoLens doesn't ask an LLM to make up market numbers.

The system separates:

  1. Understanding What did you actually ask?
  2. Planning What analysis is required?
  3. Data What does CoinMarketCap report?
  4. Analysis What do the numbers show?
  5. Verification Does the result satisfy the request?
  6. Response What should you know?

This means the language model handles understanding and explanation, while the analytical runtime handles the actual data retrieval, calculations, and verification.

Example

You say:

When BTC drops by 2%, check the top 100 coins and tell me which ones fall the most.

KryptoLens understands:

Trigger
BTC falls 2% or more.
Universe
Top 100 assets.
Analysis
Calculate their percentage declines.
Output
Rank from largest decline to smallest.

Then it works.

KryptoLens monitors the trigger, retrieves live market data, performs the calculations, verifies the ranking, and returns the result in your Lens conversation.

  • No spreadsheet.
  • No manual scanning.
  • No custom script.
