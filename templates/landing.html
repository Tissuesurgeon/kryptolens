What is KryptoLens?

Crypto markets produce an enormous amount of information every second. Prices change. Volumes move. Market rankings shift. Assets behave differently across market conditions. Large market movements can trigger reactions across hundreds of other assets.

The problem is not simply finding data. The problem is knowing:

  • What should I look at?
  • Which assets matter?
  • What changed?
  • How significant was the change?
  • What happened around the event?
  • Which historical patterns are relevant?
  • Which assets behaved differently from the rest of the market?
  • What should I continue monitoring?

KryptoLens is designed to help answer those questions. Rather than giving you another dashboard filled with information, KryptoLens gives you a workspace where you can describe the intelligence you want.

Watch BTC and tell me when it drops more than 2%.

When BTC drops by 2%, check the top 100 cryptocurrencies and show me which ones fall the most.

Find unusual volume activity across the market.

Compare the current market environment with previous periods where BTC experienced a similar move.

KryptoLens turns these requests into structured analytical workflows. Instead of searching through dashboards, charts, news feeds, and market tables yourself, tell KryptoLens what you want to know in natural language.

KryptoLens understands your request, asks for clarification when necessary, determines what analysis is required, retrieves relevant market data from CoinMarketCap, performs the analysis, verifies the results, and explains its findings. Because a Lens persists, your work does not disappear when the conversation ends.

How KryptoLens works

Create a Lens

A Lens is a persistent intelligence workspace. Give it a name such as:

  • Market Watch
  • BTC Reaction
  • Altcoin Monitor
  • Market Research

You don't need to configure a complicated agent or choose a specialist. Just create the Lens.

Tell KryptoLens what you want

Describe your request naturally. You don't need to know which API endpoint to use or which analytical method is required.

I want to know which top 100 coins react the most when BTC drops sharply.

KryptoLens interprets the request and determines what information is missing.

KryptoLens asks questions when necessary

Good analysis depends on a clear definition of the task. If your request is ambiguous, KryptoLens does not simply guess.

What should count as a sharp BTC drop — 2%, 5%, or another threshold?

Should the analysis consider the top 50, top 100, or the entire market?

You answer the question, and the Lens continues from there. Clarification becomes part of the analysis rather than a separate configuration screen.

KryptoLens builds the investigation

Once the request is clear, KryptoLens determines what capabilities are required. You do not need to choose an analyst. The system can dynamically use capabilities such as:

Market analysis
Understand current market conditions, rankings, prices, market capitalization, volume, and other market metrics.
Anomaly analysis
Identify unusual movements, abnormal activity, significant changes, or assets behaving differently from their surrounding market.
Reaction analysis
Study how one asset or market event relates to the behavior of other assets. When BTC falls 2%, which of the top 100 coins fall the most?
Historical analysis
Compare current market conditions with historical market data and previous market behavior.
Discovery
Find assets, categories, market segments, or opportunities that match a particular analytical request.
Regime analysis
Analyze broader market conditions and identify the characteristics of the current market environment.

These capabilities work behind the scenes. You interact with KryptoLens, not a collection of separate bots.

The analysis happens deterministically

AI is useful for understanding what you mean. Market calculations should not depend on an AI model inventing numbers. KryptoLens separates those responsibilities.

The intelligence layer can determine what you want, what data is needed, and what analysis should be performed. The analytical runtime handles fetching data, calculating changes, filtering assets, sorting results, comparing observations, aggregating information, and validating results.

List the biggest drops first.

That request is handled by deterministic ranking logic rather than asking a language model to decide which number is larger.

Results are verified before they are presented

An explanation should be grounded in the evidence that produced it. Before a result is presented, the system can verify:

  • Whether the required data was available
  • Whether the requested assets were actually analyzed
  • Whether calculations completed successfully
  • Whether ranking and filtering rules were applied
  • Whether the result contains the expected evidence
  • Whether missing data was incorrectly treated as zero

If the available CoinMarketCap data is insufficient, KryptoLens should say so rather than inventing an answer.

KryptoLens explains the result

The final response turns the verified analytical result into an understandable explanation. Instead of exposing a collection of API responses, you receive the relevant findings and supporting evidence.
