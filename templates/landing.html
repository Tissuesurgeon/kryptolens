What is KryptoLens?

Crypto markets produce an enormous amount of information every second. Prices change. Volumes move. Market rankings shift. Assets behave differently across market conditions. Large market movements can trigger reactions across hundreds of other assets.

The problem is not simply finding data. The problem is knowing:

  • What should I look at?
  • Which assets matter?
  • What changed?
  • How significant was the change?
  • What happened around the event?
  • Which historical patterns are relevant?
  • Which assets behaved differently from the rest of the market?
  • What should I continue monitoring?

KryptoLens is designed to help answer those questions. Rather than giving you another dashboard filled with information, it gives you a workspace where you describe the intelligence you want. It understands the request, asks when something is missing, retrieves CoinMarketCap data, analyzes it, verifies the result, and explains the findings.

How KryptoLens works

You don't need to configure a complicated agent or choose a specialist. Create an Analyst, tell it what matters, and the investigation stays in that workspace.

Create an Analyst

Tell KryptoLens what you want

Market, anomaly, reaction, historical, discovery, and regime analysis are selected behind the scenes. You interact with one Lens.
KryptoLens retrieves live market data
Listings, prices, capitalization, volume, rankings, historical quotes, categories, and global metrics come from CoinMarketCap, chosen for the task.
The analysis happens deterministically
The intelligence layer decides what to analyze. The runtime fetches, calculates, filters, sorts, compares, aggregates, and validates. “List the biggest drops first” is ranking logic, not a language model guessing which number is larger.
Results are verified before they are presented
Availability, coverage, calculations, ranking rules, evidence, and missing values are checked. If CoinMarketCap data is insufficient, KryptoLens says so.
KryptoLens explains the result
Findings stay attached to the trigger, the universe, the analysis, the ranking, and the inspectable evidence.
